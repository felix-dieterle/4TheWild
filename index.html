📍 Location

🗺 Plan a Trip

Register your intention to visit the current map area. Other users planning the same area will see a lower silence score, helping everyone avoid unintentional overcrowding.

🧭 Navigation

Enter a destination and select your travel mode to calculate a route from your current location (or the map centre). The selected mode is also used by the reachability filter below.

🚌 Transportation

Select how you travel to determine which areas are reachable. Spots more than 200 m from any accessible route will be greyed out on the map (needs zoom level 12 or closer) and excluded from the quietest-spots list. Select multiple modes to combine reachability.

Analyze Area

Pan & zoom the map to your region of interest, then click Analyze to compute the noise heatmap.

    🌲 Nature & Terrain

    Vegetation (forests, scrubland, wetlands) dampens sound and is factored into the heatmap. The terrain layer shows rough tracks and demanding hiking routes that indicate low accessibility. Built-up density uses land use (residential, retail, commercial) and nearby POIs (shops, restaurants, offices) to mark crowded places such as shopping streets and town centres.

    Road Noise Weights

    Select a preset profile or adjust individual road-type weights manually.

    🚆 Railway Noise

    Railway lines (trains, trams, subways) are included as an additional noise source. Disable the toggle to exclude them from the analysis.
